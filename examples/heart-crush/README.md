# HEART CRUSH! test fixture

This is an example of the proposed v1 manifest and chart structure for implementing a loader. The notes and 120 BPM are placeholders, not a transcription of the song, and have not been synchronized by listening.

The manifest expects `audio/song.wav`, which is not included. Convert your local MP3 to PCM WAV and put it there to complete the package. The original MP3 is in your Downloads folder. Do not commit or distribute the commercial audio without permission.

The current game has no map loader, audio playback or gameplay, so this fixture cannot yet be played. Once those exist, check audio duration and note bounds and adjust the BPM, offset and note times by ear.
